# Peoples, faiths, and homelands

Working draft for discussion. The ten entries come from the Era of Embers peoples chapter. The origin stories, cults, and homeland suggestions here are proposals. The Barrowbound return through an individual choice after death. The identification of Hel as the one who offers that choice follows her place as Hostess in the setting's pantheon. The old kingdoms belong to the history of the homelands. Present communities are mixed and can inherit traditions from several of them.

## Sources for the ancestry chapter

The ten names and their distinguishing features come from _Peoples of the Ice_ in the Era of Embers character chapters. _Cosmogony and Theological Foundations_ supplies the making of Midgard from Ymir and the kinship of the Jötnar. _The Pantheon_ gives Hel her title of Hostess, Hallinskidi his watch, Forseti his truce, and Tyr the prohibition on swearing for another. _The Architecture of Decline_, _The Atlas of Svellheim_, and the coastal, interior, and northern gazetteer journals in svellheim-world locate those traditions in the inhabited world and its present winter.

The seven eggs of the Ormá replace the descent from sleepers in the earlier character text. Runa, Yrsa, Una, the hill-wife's weaving, and the testimonies at Urdvatn are inventions for this draft. The obligation that follows death belongs to the individual who makes the promise. The draft leaves open what Hel does when that promise is fulfilled.

## Geographic anchors

Use the author’s hand-drawn map alongside the written atlas. See [the map and reference check](../maps/README.md).

The Era of Embers atlas and hex reference place Hrafnborg on the southern coast, the Singing Islands offshore to the west, the Greenweald's remains and the Bone-Fields in the Interior, Midgard's Shield across the Interior, and the White Waste and Throat of the World farther north. Use these as anchors for discussion. The historical homeland boundaries and the original shrine routes need their own evidence. High-Hall is an existing Deepforged landmark. An old kingdom's name, extent, and capital remain open.

## Hearthborn

For the creation narrative, see [The Hearthborn of the Fjord Clay](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-hearthborn-of-the-fjord-clay).

The proposed homeland follows the inner shores of the Splintered Fjords from Ketilsfjord past Hrafnborg's harbor to the Saltá at Saltvik. Early villages use landing beaches and stream mouths, with houses and stores above the reach of the tide. They share access to fishing grounds and salt, and in larger places several households maintain a common hall. Hrafnborg grew around its flame spring and is a mixed city in the present. The first village names and the date of the earliest settlements remain open.

## Cragbound

For the creation narrative, see [The Cragbound, Kin of the Jötnar](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-cragbound-kin-of-the-jötnar).

The settlement draft gives the larger communities stone houses and halls built on high shelves near water and summer grazing. More exposed parts of their routes have outlying shelters. Hay, stored food, fuel brought above the tree line, and exchange with lower settlements make year-round life possible. The precise extent and seats of the old Cragbound homeland are open. Titan's Stair and Stairwatch are geographic anchors for the mountain routes, and the entrances to the Deepforged holds are possible points of contact.

## Tonttu

For the creation narrative, see [The Tonttu of the Living Wood](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-tonttu-of-the-living-wood). The relationship of the water springs to the flame springs remains open.

The villages follow the Askfoss and the forest springs. The draft describes linked clearings, high storehouses, shared plant beds and gathering grounds, and trade along the forest margins. The settlement names and the extent of the old Tonttu homeland remain open. The Green Heart is a place of uncertain survival in accounts from the Age of Embers.

## Lumenkin

For the creation narrative, see [The Lumenkin at the Crossing of the Veil](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-lumenkin-at-the-crossing-of-the-veil).

The proposed homeland is the eastern coast below the White Waste, along the seaward slopes of the Frozen Steppe and south toward the Hornvatn outlet at present-day Rindgate. The first lasting settlements occupy sheltered fishing coves with fresh water and terraces, linked by signal fires and upland paths. Their records of weather, tides, and travel give the later Lumenkin tradition of scholarship a local beginning. The homeland's historical name, its earliest settlement names, and the extent of the coast lost to ice remain open.

## Veilfolk

For the creation narrative, see [The Veilfolk Under the Hill](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-veilfolk-under-the-hill).

The proposed homeland is the coastal hill country between the lower Saltá and the Grátelva, inland of present-day Thornwall, Strandvik, and Grátholm. Its first burrow-halls are built into well-drained slopes near springs, grazing, and routes between coast and Interior. Individual halls face a shared yard in the larger settlements. The historical names of those halls, the exact homeland boundaries, and the identities of the riders in the founding story remain open.

## Ormsbani

For the creation narrative, see [The Ormsbani and the Wyrm of the Ford](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-ormsbani-and-the-wyrm-of-the-ford).

The proposed homeland lies on the upper Ormá from the Djupá divide downstream toward the mouth of the Kolbekk. Its oath-halls occupy ground above the fords, with farms on the river terraces and landings and stock pens nearer the water. The halls give inherited obligations a public setting, and maintaining a crossing serves both the local households and travelers. The oldest hall names, the homeland's historical name, and the historical reach of each ford household remain open.

## Barrowbound

For personal accounts of death, Hel's offer, and the oaths of the Returned, see [The Barrowbound Before Hel](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-barrowbound-before-hel).

The early communities bring together Returned from several homelands, alongside some of their living kin. Urdvatn's eastern shore is the proposed location of their oldest remembered refuge. The common room, fishing landing, and exchanges with the farms around the lake give the settlement a livelihood and a way to help newcomers reach their kin. Urdvatn can be called a homeland by the people who made a home there. Its refuge gathers people whose first homes lie throughout Svellheim. The date the settlement began and its relationship to the builders of the ring fortress remain open.

## Cinderbound

For the creation narrative, see [The Cinderbound and the Judgment at the Spring](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-cinderbound-and-the-judgment-at-the-spring).

The proposed homeland is the lower volcanic terraces on the southern approaches to the Throat of the World, below the Steam-Oasis. Early villages use the springs to warm gardens and workrooms and trade with settlements farther south. The Steam-Oasis retains its role as neutral ground. The oldest village names, the boundaries of the homeland, and the date of the first shared hearing remain open.

## Regional development

Develop a homeland around a named place, a reason people could live there, and a custom that survives in the settlements formed after the wars. Ask what each community remembers about the shrine roads. A local account might remember a road as the way to a spring, the route taken by the dead, or a promise of safe passage. Reconcile overlaps once the original map and the homeland traditions can be read together. Return to the wars after those decisions.

## Decisions to settle from the chapter draft

The gazetteer chapter develops the origin accounts and customary practices above as working material. The strongest geographic anchors are the hearth traditions of the Splintered Fjords, the Deepforged works in Midgard's Shield, the Cragbound high approaches, and the Tonttu association with old woods and warm water.

- The Deepforged lived deep underground and the Cragbound lived in the high mountain ranges. The timing of the Deepforged settlement above ground, their first contact with the Cragbound, and any duties they shared where high paths met the deep holds remain open.
- Decide how closely to place the old Tonttu communities and Ashmarked winter ranges around the former Greenweald.
- The eastern coves below the White Waste are the proposed Lumenkin homeland. Confirm its name and northern extent.
- The coastal hills between the lower Saltá and the Grátelva are the proposed Veilfolk homeland. The upper Ormá between the Djupá divide and the vicinity of the Kolbekk confluence is the proposed Ormsbani homeland. The names of its oldest oath-halls remain open.
- The Barrowbound come from every people. The proposed refuge on Urdvatn received people from several homelands. Its founding date and relationship to the ring fortress remain open.
- The lower terraces below the Steam-Oasis are the proposed Cinderbound homeland. The oldest settlement names and the date of the judgment remain open.
