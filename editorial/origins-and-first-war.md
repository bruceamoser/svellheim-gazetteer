# Origins and the First War

These are working proposals for collaboration. The order of events, the disputed place, and the people involved are open.

## Origin traditions

Give each people a story of its beginning, a reason for its relationship with particular gods or spirits, a place where that relationship first mattered, and a custom still practiced. A homeland can preserve several tellings. An origin story can help explain a people's oldest obligations while leaving room for anyone to worship differently.

### Hearthborn

In the [fjord account](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-hearthborn-of-the-fjord-clay), Frigg puts her fire into the hands of the first child while she digs out the others. The right of a stranger to warm up and the duty of witnesses who heard what a host promised both have a place in a dispute over an old hall.

### Deepforged

The [deep holds](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-deepforged-beneath-the-roots) preserve both the painting from the roots and a descent into the earth. The first woman answers the knocking of kin behind stone. A duty to answer a call carries weight even where a neighboring clan controls the passage. The builders’ oaths to Tyr can draw another house into such a dispute.

### Cragbound

In the [high-range account](../gazetteer/05-ancestries.md#the-cragbound-kin-of-the-jötnar), Skadi shows Runa’s children how to find their way back to their mother. Those who guide in the heights owe aid to anyone who gives the old call. Closing a path in winter could breach that duty even when a ruler commands it.

## The First War

The proposed opening is the [quarrel at Urdvatn](../gazetteer/03-history.md#the-quarrel-at-urdvatn). Reedbank and Cairn Ness are proposed names for early communities on opposite shores. Individual returns predate the refuge. Reedbank offers guest-right under Frigg. Cairn Ness understands burial as entrusting a guest to Hel and fears what creditors could demand of the Returned. The fate of a Returned person after fulfillment remains uncertain.

The First War is called the War of the Dead. The continuation begins with the lintel oath to Cairn Ness and the ford-keeper's promise of an oar to Reedbank. The first archer remains unknown. Reprisals at the lake reach the river kingdoms through a killing at an oath-hall threshold. The war reaches the other homelands through older obligations, interrupted trade, and the breaking of local sanctuary. The release of the defense oaths, defeat of Vargeld Hollowking, and terms for the Returned at Reedbank close the proposed history. Vargeld’s hidden survival belongs to the development of the Second War.
