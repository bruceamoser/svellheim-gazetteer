## Reference check

- North: White Waste and its ice-front marks, Frozen City, Surt's Forge, the other calderas, Steam-Oasis, Throat of the World, Aurora Spire, and Vanaheim's Joy in its cloud.
- Interior: Midgard's Shield, High-Hall, Titan's Stair, Iron Bogs, Myrkwood, Walking Stones, methane vents, Ashen Woods, Kolhavn, Charcoal Keep, Askveld, Green Heart, Beinvaka, Frozen Steppe, Bone-Fields, Ivory Tower, and Rindgate.
- Waters inland: Myrvatn, Asksjø, Hornvatn, Urdvatn, Djupá, Saltá, Kolbekk, Ormá, Grátelva, Askfoss, and the drawn branches and mouths.
- Coast and islands: the Splintered Fjords, Chain-Boom, Salt-Pines, Hrafnborg, Ketilsfjord, Saltvik, Thornwall, Strandvik, Grátholm, Ormsund, Brimstead, Weeping Coast, Mast-Forest, wrecks, Siren's Rock, Singing Islands, Echoing Caves, Eldøy, Vent-Gardens, and smaller unnamed islands.

The Mast-Forest is the wreck-strewn water off the Weeping Coast near Grátholm. Move its label offshore for the next edition. Retain the broad inland woodland motif without a name until its identity is confirmed.

## Typesetting and geography review

Use the atlas forms Midgard's Shield, Titan's Stair, Steam-Oasis, Vanaheim's Joy, Echoing Caves, Splintered Fjords, Bone-Fields, Salt-Pines, and Chain-Boom. Restore the written diacritics in Eldøy, Grátholm, Asksjø, Djupá, Saltá, Ormá, and Grátelva. Use Ketilsfjord, Hrafnborg, Myrkwood, Beinvaka, Rindgate, Strandvik, and Brimstead for the lettered places. The generated color proof introduced additional errors. Set labels from the written references and the author's corrections.

| Feature | What to resolve |
| --- | --- |
| Djupá and Saltá | The drawn north-south channel forks inland of Hrafnborg. One arm goes toward Hrafnborg and the other to Saltvik. The atlas calls Djupá an Ormá tributary and Saltá a coastal stream at Saltvik. See the author-confirmed branching below. |
| Grátelva and Ormá | On the sketch, the Grátelva appears to share an upstream junction with the Ormá before running to the coast near Grátholm. The atlas describes it as a short river rising in the coastal hills behind Grátholm. Preserve the Ormá mouth at Ormsund and use the independent source confirmed below. |
| Rindgate | Its dot is drawn close to the eastern shoreline beside Hornvatn. The atlas describes a pass-town at the eastern foot of the Shield. Retain its location and add the Hornvatn outlet. |
| Frozen Steppe | The sketch gives this its own label above Bone-Fields. The atlas describes the Bone-Fields themselves as a frozen steppe. The label describes the whole outlined plateau. |
| Vanaheim's Joy | The drawing places it in a cloud in the northeast. The atlas treats the valley as legendary. In the digital edition, keep the cloud as a legend vignette without marking a precise location unless the author confirms one. |
| Inland southern woodland | Keep the woodland visible from the original. Its identity or name is open. |

The hex reference includes places absent from the sketch, including Stairwatch and Grove of Upsal. Stairwatch and Grove of Upsal are approved additions. Check the completed map against the drawing and this review before export.

The trace preserves the artwork's relative placement and original lettering. Thresholding may simplify light pencil marks and leaves some small paper specks. The file is an ink proof, with no surveyed scale, typeset label layer, or inferred historical borders.

## Author-confirmed geography

- The Ormá rises in the mountains near High-Hall and branches into the Ormá and Djupá. The Djupá later branches into the Djupá and Saltá. The drawing already shows the two forks.
- The Grátelva rises independently in the mountains below the Ashen Woods and reaches the coast near Grátholm.
- A small unnamed river runs from Hornvatn through Rindgate to the sea.
- The full outlined area is the frozen steppe or elevated plateau. The Bone-Fields lie within it.
- Add Stairwatch immediately west of Titan's Stair, and place the Grove of Upsal southeast of Eldøy, following the hex reference. Their placement on the sketch is approximate.

The [geography proof](svellheim-geography-proof.svg) overlays these changes on the ink trace for review. The original ink trace remains the comparison reference. Full label typesetting and visual treatment belong to the print edition.
